# Post-service Follow-up

## Content pattern

Thank-you, sermon title and summary, key scriptures, weekly reflection, prayer points, practical challenge, social/video links, and upcoming programs.

## Modes

1. Prepared in advance by pastor or communications team.
2. AI-assisted from a sermon transcript or approved notes.

```text
Service ends
→ attendance finalized
→ draft prepared/generated
→ factual and theological review
→ audience and links reviewed
→ authorized sender approves
→ email scheduled/sent
```

AI output never bypasses review by default. The demo simulates generation, review, and send without uploading or transmitting data.
