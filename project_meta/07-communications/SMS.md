# SMS

SMS is useful for urgent or inclusive reach but is a variable-cost channel. Unicode and multipart messages can consume multiple billing units.

## Send safeguards

- Show audience size, estimated units, and estimated cost before approval.
- Enforce monthly organization budget and approval thresholds.
- Segment rather than defaulting to the whole church.
- Use short content and track opt-out instructions where required.
- Prefer push or email fallback when appropriate.

Provider choice, sender-ID rules, delivery receipt semantics, Nigerian regulatory review, and international behavior remain undecided.
