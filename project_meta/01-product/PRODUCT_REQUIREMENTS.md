# Product Requirements

## Functional requirement groups

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| PR-01 | Isolate all organization-owned records and settings | Foundational |
| PR-02 | Manage people from visitor through member lifecycle | Pilot |
| PR-03 | Assign and track follow-up ownership and outcomes | Pilot |
| PR-04 | Manage events, recurring programs, and attendance | Pilot |
| PR-05 | Send segmented push, SMS, and email communications | Pilot |
| PR-06 | Configure deterministic automations with human review gates | Pilot |
| PR-07 | Support giving funds, projects, ministry needs, and in-kind offers | Pilot |
| PR-08 | Provide granular finance permissions and audit trails | Foundational |
| PR-09 | Compose organization-specific roles from canonical permissions and scopes | Foundational |
| PR-10 | Configure modules independently from RBAC | Foundational |
| PR-11 | Assist approved content workflows with measurable, disableable AI | Later pilot |
| PR-12 | Provide dashboards, exports, audit views, and operational reports | Pilot/later |

## Cross-cutting requirements

- Human-readable state, ownership, timestamps, and error recovery.
- Consent and communication preferences respected per channel.
- Accessibility target WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Immutable audit records for sensitive actions in production.
- Cost preview and limits for variable-cost channels and AI.
- Production technology choices remain open unless recorded in the decision log.
