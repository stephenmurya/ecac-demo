# MVP Scope

## Pilot candidate

- Organization and branch setup.
- Member and visitor directory with consent and preferences.
- Follow-up assignment, outcomes, reminders, and conversion.
- Events, recurring programs, basic attendance, and home cells.
- Push-first communications plus selective SMS and email.
- Deterministic automations for common workflows.
- Giving funds and project progress, subject to payment design validation.
- Ministry need submission, item approval, and in-kind offers.
- Organization roles composed from canonical permissions.
- Audit coverage for role, finance, communication, and settings changes.

## Later or optional

Community discussions, trivia, live audio, leaderboards, advanced volunteer scheduling, AI knowledge assistant, platform billing, and full SaaS self-service.

## Exit criteria before production pilot

Tenant isolation tests, threat model, data retention policy, consent review, provider decisions, finance reconciliation rules, incident process, backups, monitoring, support ownership, and acceptance testing are all required. None is satisfied by this demo.
