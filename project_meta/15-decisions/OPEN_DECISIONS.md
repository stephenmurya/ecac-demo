# Open Decisions

## Product and pilot

- Pilot church, branches, cohort, duration, owners, success thresholds, and rollout order.
- Exact MVP cut, community/trivia/live-audio priority, and member-mobile scope.
- Visitor consent fields, membership lifecycle rules, household/minor handling, and pastoral-note policy.
- Finance separation of duties, public project defaults, receipts, offline giving, and in-kind valuation.

## Technology and providers

- Production web/mobile framework, cloud, database, identity, queue, storage, search, monitoring, and analytics.
- Shared versus isolated tenant storage and higher-isolation tiers.
- Payment, SMS, email, push, transcription, and AI providers and data terms.
- SESSIONS reuse strategy after an explicit code/architecture assessment.

## Governance and commercial

- Data retention/deletion, privacy jurisdictions, minors/safeguarding, incident response, and support access.
- SaaS plans, entitlements, billing, service levels, support model, and organization offboarding.
- AI source approval, model evaluation, theological review roles, and crisis/escalation policy.

The demo’s labels, sample roles, and Basecoat visual choices do not resolve these production decisions.
