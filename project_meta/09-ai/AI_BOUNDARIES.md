# AI Boundaries

- Do not position the assistant as a pastor, spiritual authority, counselor, or source of emergency care.
- Do not let model output authorize payments, change permissions, determine attendance consequences, or send church-wide theology without review.
- Clearly distinguish retrieved approved material from generated synthesis.
- Minimize personal and pastoral data in prompts; define provider retention and training terms before use.
- Show uncertainty and source gaps rather than fabricate church policy.
- Provide human correction, regeneration, and cancellation paths.

Safeguarding, crisis-language handling, translation review, copyright of sermon materials, and model/provider data residency require policy decisions.
