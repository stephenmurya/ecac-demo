# AI Features

Appropriate AI assistance includes drafting announcements, rewriting for tone, sermon/transcript summarization, study questions, content repurposing, translation drafts, administrative summaries, classification, knowledge search, and post-service email drafting.

Deterministic software remains responsible for dates, schedules, segmentation, permissions, triggers, audience rules, budgets, and provider delivery.

AI is an organization-level feature that can be disabled. Individual AI tasks require `ai.use`; configuration and source governance require `ai.manage`. Usage is metered by tenant, task, model, and cost.
