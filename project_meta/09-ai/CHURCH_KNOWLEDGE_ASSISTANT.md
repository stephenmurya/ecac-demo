# Church Knowledge Assistant

The future assistant answers from organization-approved sources such as the statement of faith, constitution, membership handbook, sermons, Bible-study materials, approved teachings, and ministry documentation.

## Answer contract

1. Resolve organization and user access.
2. Retrieve only approved, accessible source passages.
3. Answer with source references and distinguish quotation, summary, and inference.
4. State when the approved library does not answer the question.
5. Route pastoral, safeguarding, crisis, or policy-sensitive questions to people.

Source approval, versioning, withdrawal, indexing, permission inheritance, and answer evaluation are required before launch.
