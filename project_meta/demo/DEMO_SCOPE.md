# Demo Scope

## Purpose

Give church leadership a credible, connected view of future operations without implying production readiness.

## Interactive

- Overview metrics and attention items.
- Visitor selection, timeline, contact, scheduling, cell assignment, and conversion.
- Automation list and trigger-to-send explanation.
- Admin/member view switch and guided giving review.
- Project progress, monetary contribution, full funding, supply/new-or-owned in-kind paths.
- Ministry need submission, review, request changes/reject/approve, and publication to member needs.
- AI-assisted Sunday follow-up creation, editing, review, and send state.
- Member directory with household, church-life, and message-preference details.
- Follow-up scheduling, ownership, reassignment, and outcomes.
- Project-specific giving, church-admin project creation, and offline contribution entry.
- Local persistence and complete demo reset.

## Deliberately inactive

Events, Home Cells, Attendance, Communications, Ministries, Reports, and Team Access remain muted in the navigation until they are built.

## Simulated only

All identities, money, delivery, approvals, AI output, and dates are fictional/browser-local. No network service is called at runtime; locally vendored Basecoat UI provides components. No action sends a message, moves money, uploads a file, or creates a real member.
