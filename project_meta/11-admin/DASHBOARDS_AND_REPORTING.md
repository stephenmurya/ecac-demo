# Dashboards and Reporting

Dashboards answer operational questions: what needs attention, who owns it, what changed, and where intervention is needed. Reports support trends and accountable exports.

Candidate measures include attendance by occurrence/branch, visitor conversion stages, follow-up timeliness, home-cell participation, campaign delivery, giving by fund/project, need fulfillment, automation failures, SMS/AI usage, and access changes.

Metric definitions, date windows, timezone, exclusions, privacy thresholds, and source freshness must be visible. Aggregates must not leak restricted donor or pastoral identity.
