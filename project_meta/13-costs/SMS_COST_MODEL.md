# SMS Cost Model

Planning unit cost: **₦8**. Actual provider pricing, routes, taxes, Unicode, and multipart messages may differ.

```text
Annual SMS cost = members × messages/member/month × 12 × unit cost
```

| Members | 1 SMS/member/month | 5 SMS/member/month | 10 SMS/member/month |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 500 | ₦48,000 | ₦240,000 | ₦480,000 |
| 1,000 | ₦96,000 | ₦480,000 | ₦960,000 |
| 2,500 | ₦240,000 | ₦1,200,000 | ₦2,400,000 |
| 5,000 | ₦480,000 | ₦2,400,000 | ₦4,800,000 |

Segmentation means most SMS should not go to every member.
