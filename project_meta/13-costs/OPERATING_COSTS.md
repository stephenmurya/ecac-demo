# Operating Costs

Present future operating costs conservatively and separate fixed-ish platform costs from variable usage.

Potential categories: cloud/backend, storage, email, SMS, AI runtime, domains, monitoring, logs, payment transaction fees, and third-party integrations.

Previous planning context suggests a non-SMS envelope around **₦500,000–₦900,000/year** initially. At 1,000 members and 5 SMS/member/month, planned SMS alone is **₦480,000/year**. A cautious combined illustration is **₦1.2m–₦1.8m/year**, depending on architecture and usage. Larger SMS-heavy deployments can exceed this.

These are planning scenarios, not quotes. Provider selection, load tests, data volumes, support, redundancy, FX, taxes, and transaction fees can materially change them.
