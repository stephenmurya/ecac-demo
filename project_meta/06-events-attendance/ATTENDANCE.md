# Attendance

Attendance associates a person or guest with a program occurrence, method, timestamp, and correction history. Potential capture modes include assisted roster, QR, PIN, self check-in, and import.

## Occurrence state

`Scheduled → Open → Capturing → Review → Finalized → Corrected`

Finalization freezes ordinary edits; corrections require reason and audit. Duplicate check-ins, guests without profiles, late sync, proxy check-in, and privacy of individual attendance need production rules.

Attendance analytics inform care and planning. They do not autonomously send messages about someone’s absence.
