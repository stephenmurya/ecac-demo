# Human in the Loop

Human review is required when context, dignity, theology, finance, or broad reputational impact outweighs the benefit of immediate automation.

## Default review gates

- Church-wide AI-generated theological communication.
- Pastoral outreach inferred from attendance or care signals.
- Ministry need publication and item-level approval.
- High-cost SMS campaigns over organization threshold.
- Refunds, reconciliation exceptions, and payment setting changes.
- Permission elevation and last-owner transitions.

Approvals record who reviewed, what exact version they saw, decision, time, and optional reason. Editing approved content invalidates approval when material.
