# Role Examples

| Role | Example permissions | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Organization Owner | Governance and settings plus delegated operational access | Organization |
| Branch Administrator | Members, visitors, events, attendance, communications | One branch |
| Follow-up Coordinator | Visitors view/manage and follow-ups manage | Organization or branch |
| Home-cell Leader | Members view, attendance manage, follow-ups manage | One home cell |
| Communications Lead | Templates and permitted channel sends | Organization |
| Finance Viewer | `finance.view_summary` only | Organization |
| Finance Officer | Transactions, reconciliation, export | Organization |
| Ministry Leader | Group/member view, events, needs submission concept | One ministry |

These are templates, not hard-coded roles. Finance donor identity, refunds, payment settings, and exports remain independently grantable.
