# Visitors

Visitors and first timers are pre-membership people with consent-aware follow-up. Registration may come from a form, assisted desk entry, event import, or later integration.

## Minimum useful record

Name, safe contact route, first-visit date/program, branch, interests, how they heard about the church, consent/preferences, and optional locality. Collect only what supports care.

## Statuses

`New → Welcome sent → Assigned → In follow-up → Connected → Converted to member / Closed`

Repeated visits should enrich one person record rather than create duplicates. Visitor visibility is scoped; free-form sensitive notes are not broadly searchable or exportable.
