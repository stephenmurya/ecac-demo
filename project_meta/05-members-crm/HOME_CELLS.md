# Home Cells

Home cells are organization-owned small groups associated with leaders, branch, meeting schedule, capacity, and carefully controlled location data.

## Capabilities

Directory and recommendation, assignment/request, weekly reminders with the correct meeting details, attendance, leader report, pastoral follow-up, and membership history.

## Privacy

Exact residential locations should be visible only to eligible members and leaders. Public discovery may show approximate area. Leader access is scoped to the cell and does not imply organization-wide member access.

## Lifecycle

`Proposed → Active → Paused → Merged/Closed`

Capacity, geography-based recommendation, minors, co-leadership, and member-request approval are open product decisions.
