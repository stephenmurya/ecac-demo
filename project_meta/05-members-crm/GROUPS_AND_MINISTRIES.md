# Groups and Ministries

Groups are flexible collections. Ministries/departments are operational teams with leadership, rosters, programs, volunteer roles, and the ability to submit needs.

| Concept | Typical examples | Distinct behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Ministry/department | Music, Welfare, Media | Operational ownership, needs, schedules |
| Group | Young Adults, New Members | Audience and participation |
| Home cell | Airport Road Cell | Pastoral locality, meetings, attendance |

Membership can be requested, invited, assigned, approved, paused, or ended. Role assignment is separate from group membership: being in Music Ministry does not automatically grant ministry administration.
