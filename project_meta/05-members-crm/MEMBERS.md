# Members

The member record is a people-and-relationship profile, not merely an app account. A member can exist without signing in, relate to multiple branches/groups, and hold scoped leadership assignments.

## Core information

- Preferred and legal/display names, contact methods, household relationships.
- Primary and secondary branch relationships.
- Membership lifecycle, dates, and source.
- Ministries, groups, home cells, tags, interests, and service preferences.
- Communication consent and per-channel preferences.
- Restricted pastoral-care indicators with separate authorization.

## Lifecycle

`Prospective → New member → Active → Inactive → Transferred/Archived`

Lifecycle changes retain history and reason. “Inactive” must not be inferred solely from attendance. Duplicate detection, household modeling, children/safeguarding, data correction, and deletion policies remain open design areas.
