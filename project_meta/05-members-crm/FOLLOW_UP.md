# Follow-up

Follow-up is a case-and-task workflow with a responsible person, due date, contact context, and structured outcome.

```text
Visitor registered
→ welcome message
→ follow-up worker assigned
→ reminder created
→ contact outcome recorded
→ invited to service or cell
→ member conversion or continued nurture
```

## Outcomes

Reached, no answer, wrong contact, prefers another channel, requested call later, visited again, joined cell, declined further contact, and safety/escalation. Outcomes can create a next task.

## Guardrails

- Respect opt-out immediately.
- Avoid automatic messages that expose attendance patterns.
- Separate operational notes from restricted pastoral notes.
- Reassignment, overdue escalation, and closure are audited.
