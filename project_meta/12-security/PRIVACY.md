# Privacy

The platform will process identity, contact, attendance, group, pastoral, communication, and finance data. Privacy design follows purpose limitation, data minimization, role/scoped access, retention, consent/preferences, secure export, and accountable deletion.

Production requires a data inventory, lawful-basis review, privacy notice, processor/provider review, retention schedule, subject-request process, breach process, cross-border assessment, and policy for children/minors. These are not completed by the demo.
