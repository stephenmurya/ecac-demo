# Goals and Non-goals

## Goals

| Horizon | Goal |
|---|---|
| Current task | Document the full product and demonstrate selected leadership flows in a static prototype |
| Pilot | Validate visitor care, communications, attendance, needs, giving, and post-service workflows with one church |
| Future | Support multiple independent organizations with scoped RBAC, configuration, and strict isolation |

## Non-goals for the current demo

- No production application, backend, tenant enforcement, authentication, database, APIs, payments, messaging, AI calls, or deployment automation.
- No modification or integration of SESSIONS.
- No claim of production readiness, security validation, accounting completeness, or cost certainty.

## Product non-goals

- Replace church leadership, pastoral discernment, or human approval of sensitive communication.
- Let organizations invent arbitrary low-level permissions.
- Treat client-side payment state as proof of funds.
- Make every module available or visible to every role.
